Description

Pasta carbonara is a classic Roman pasta dish made with penne pasta, meat, eggs, cheese, salt, and black peppe

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Meat
  • Eggs
  • Cheese
  • Black Peper
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Boil the Pasta
  2. Render the Meat
  3. Mix the Eggs and Cheese sauce
  4. Temper the Eggs
  5. Combine Pasta and Meat
  6. Add the Egg Mixture
  7. Adjust consistency
  8. Finish and Serve
