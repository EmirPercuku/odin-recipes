Description

A classic homemade pepperoni pizza combines a crisp-yet-chewy crust, a tangy tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and spiced pepperon

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Pizza Souce
  • Cheese
  • Pepperoni Topping

Steps

  1. Prepare the Dough
  2. Shape the Crust
  3. Add the Souce and Cheese
  4. Top with Pepperoni
  5. Bake and Serve
